Fresher hiring in India

  • 42,000+ — fresh graduates TCS alone planned to hire in FY26, keeping it India's largest fresher recruiter. (TCS NQT hiring; Let's Code hiring calendar)
  • Two hiring peaks dominate the Indian fresher year: July–October (campus season: TCS NQT cycle 1, Wipro Elite, Capgemini) and February–April (off-campus: TCS NQT cycle 2, Infosys off-campus). (Let's Code)
  • 6–10 calls — a typical Indian job switch across two or three companies in parallel (screening + technical + managerial + HR rounds each), which is why per-session pricing on interview tools runs out mid-loop. (Interview Sarthi analysis of standard mass-recruiter processes; see our company guides)

AI in the Indian job search

  • 100 million — weekly ChatGPT users in India as of early 2026, OpenAI's second-largest market worldwide. (OpenAI via TechRepublic, Feb 2026)
  • India was Google's first market outside the US to get AI Mode in Search (June 2025), later available without sign-in and in Hindi — Indian job seekers now routinely get AI answers before web results. (Google India; Search Engine Land)
  • ~491 million — YouTube users in India, its largest market, and 71% of Indian users say they use YouTube to learn skills — including interview preparation. (Global Media Insight, 2025)

Detection, rules and the ethics debate

  • ~38.5% — share of online interviews that trigger AI-assistance suspicion flags, as claimed by interview-proctoring vendors selling detection tools (treat as a vendor figure, not independent research). (InCruiter; Fabric)
  • The rules genuinely differ case by case — from companies that prohibit any assistance to interviews where preparation tools are unremarkable; our plain-language guide to where the lines sit: Is it OK to use AI in a job interview?
  • Screen-share invisibility has real limits — capture-exclusion APIs hide overlays from Teams/Zoom/Meet, but a phone camera pointed at the monitor still sees everything, and some proctoring tools detect capture-excluded windows. (our technical explainer)
