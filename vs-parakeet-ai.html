Interview Sarthi vs Parakeet AI: the credit meter is the whole story

Parakeet AI is a capable, popular interview copilot. The difference that decides this comparison isn't AI quality — it's that Parakeet sells your interview time by the hour, and interviews don't respect meters. Updated August 2026.

The short version

Parakeet AI mainly sells credits: ~$29.50 for 3 hours of live session time (≈ ₹2,600), or an unlimited subscription at ~$74.90/month (≈ ₹6,600). Interview Sarthi is ₹399–1,999 one-time, with no hour meter, no session count, and no dollars — UPI in rupees.

Why metered time is the wrong shape for interviews

Interviews overrun, reschedule, and multiply. A "quick screening call" becomes 55 minutes; the panel adds "one more round with our architect"; two companies land offers in the same week and suddenly you're doing eight calls in five days. A credit meter turns each of those into arithmetic — do I have enough hours left? Should I save credits and go without the copilot for the HR round? That's exactly the cognitive load you bought the tool to remove. A time-period pass makes the question disappear: while the pass is active, every call is covered, however long the week gets.

Where Parakeet is genuinely better

It runs in the browser, so Mac and Linux users are covered — Sarthi is Windows-only, full stop. Credits never expire, which suits someone doing one interview every few months. And its coding-interview copilot is more established. If you're on a MacBook or you interview twice a year, Parakeet is a reasonable buy.

Where Interview Sarthi is built differently

Sarthi is a desktop app built for the Indian interview season: unlimited sessions on every pass, answers drafted from your resume in English or Hinglish, invisible in screen share on every plan including the trial, paid once in rupees by UPI. And architecturally: there is no vendor cloud — audio streams from your PC directly to Google under your own free Gemini key, so your interviews never touch our servers (we don't have any).

FAQ

What does a month of heavy interviewing cost on each?
Parakeet unlimited: ~₹6,600 (subscription). Sarthi: ₹999 one-time, nothing recurs.

Do Parakeet credits expire?
No — purchased credits keep. That's genuinely good for very occasional use; the math still favours a ₹399 week-pass by the second hour of interviews.

Can I use either on Mac?
Parakeet yes (browser). Sarthi no — Windows 10/11 only.
