FAQ

Is it visible to the interviewer?

On Windows the overlay is excluded from screen capture, so it does not appear in a shared screen or recording made through Teams, Zoom, Meet or OBS. Use headphones so your speaker audio does not reach your microphone.

Do I need to pay for the AI separately?

No. The app runs on Google’s free Gemini tier with a key you create in two minutes. Optional OpenAI or Groq keys add extra features.

What happens when my pass ends?

The app shows a renew screen. Buy a new pass, paste the new key, continue. Nothing auto-charges.

Which systems are supported?

Windows 10 (2004) and 11. Any call app that plays through your speakers: Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Webex, browser calls.

Is my data safe?

Audio goes from your PC straight to Google/OpenAI under your own key. Your resume is converted to text locally. We run no servers and store nothing.
