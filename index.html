Stays hidden

They see your screen. Not your sarthi.

Windows lets an app exclude its own window from screen capture. Interview Sarthi turns that on from the first frame, permanently. Teams, Zoom, Google Meet, Webex and OBS all receive the capture without the overlay in it.

  • No bot joins your call — it listens to your computer's own audio. No extra participant, nothing in the guest list, nothing for the interviewer to notice.
  • Invisible in screen share — full-screen or window share, the overlay is simply not there.
  • Invisible in recordings — a recorded call, or one played back later, has no trace of it.
  • Silent — it reads, never speaks. No audio goes into the call. Your mic never triggers an answer.
  • Fades to almost nothing — drag it anywhere, dim it with one key, keep it at the edge of your eye.

Honest limits: a phone camera pointed at your monitor sees everything, and a few proctoring tools detect capture-excluded windows. Use it within your interviewer's and employer's rules.

FAQ

Questions people ask before buying

Can the interviewer see it?

Not through the call. On Windows 10 (2004+) and 11 the overlay is excluded from screen capture, so it does not appear in a shared screen or a recording made through Teams, Zoom, Meet, Webex or OBS. It is visible to a phone camera pointed at your monitor and to some proctoring tools that detect capture-excluded windows.

Why does Windows say “unknown publisher”?

Because the installer is not code-signed — a certificate costs more per year than most of our passes do. It is not a detection of anything. Click More info then Run anyway; the full walkthrough with screenshots is further up this page. You can scan the file on VirusTotal first if you prefer.

Do I need to pay for the AI separately?

No. The app runs on Google's free Gemini tier with a key you create in two minutes. Optional OpenAI or Groq keys add extras, but nothing requires them.

Why is it so much cheaper than other AI interview tools?

Two reasons. Other copilots run the AI on their servers and pass that cost to you as a ₹1,750–26,000 monthly subscription. Interview Sarthi runs on your own free Gemini key, so there is no server bill to pass on. And it is priced for India — a one-time pass in rupees, paid by UPI, that simply ends instead of renewing.

Does it work for my field, or only for developers?

Any field. It reads your resume, works out your field and its vocabulary, and tunes the listener to it — marketing, finance, nursing, sales, mechanical, teaching. Coding-round features are a bonus for developers.

Hindi and Hinglish?

Yes — it hears the mix and answers in the same register, in Latin-script Hinglish so you can read it fast.

I have just one interview coming up — which pass?

The 7-Day Pass (₹399). One interview is rarely one call — there's usually a screening round, a technical round and an HR round, and dates shift. Seven days covers the whole process, including reschedules, with unlimited sessions. If more interviews land later, a month pass costs less per day.

What happens when my pass ends?

The app shows a renew screen. Buy a new pass, paste the new key, continue. Nothing auto-charges, ever.

Which systems?

Windows 10 (2004) and 11. Any call app that plays through your speakers. Use headphones so your own voice does not leak into the microphone channel.

Is my data safe?

Audio goes from your PC straight to Google (or OpenAI) under your own key. Your resume is converted locally. We run no servers and store nothing. Privacy policy.
