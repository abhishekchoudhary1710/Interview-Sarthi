ApplySarthi: the jobs that fit you, with the application already filled in.

Upload your CV once. ApplySarthi ranks tens of thousands of open jobs in India against it, writes a CV and cover letter tailored to each one, and fills the application form inside your own Chrome. You read it, fix anything you like, and press submit.

Open ApplySarthi → Sign in with your email. No password, no card.

How it works

  1. Upload your CVPDF or Word. It is read once. Every open job is then ranked by how well it matches you, and jobs asking for far more experience than you have are pushed down rather than hidden.
  2. Press Apply on a jobIt opens in a new tab. The form fills itself: your details, a draft answer to every question the form asks, and a CV reworded for that job attached as a PDF.
  3. Check, then submitAnything your CV cannot answer (expected salary, notice period, work authorisation) is highlighted for you. Answer those once in Setup and every form gets them. You solve the CAPTCHA and press the site's own submit button.

Where the jobs come from

Only jobs that are open right now, collected all day from 21 sources and checked again when they disappear. Duplicates across sites are merged, so a job on Naukri and on the employer's own page shows once, with both ways to apply.

India job sites

Naukri, LinkedIn, Indeed, Foundit, Shine, Internshala

Company career pages

750+ employers on Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby, Workday, SmartRecruiters, Oracle and more, straight from the source

Startups

Wellfound, plus a startup flag on every company under 200 people, if that is where you want to be

Remote

RemoteOK, Remotive, We Work Remotely and others, each marked if it is open to people in India

What it will not do

It never invents a fact about you. Names, employers, titles, dates, degrees and numbers are copied from your CV. The AI only rewords. A rewritten line that adds a figure your CV does not contain is thrown away and your original line is kept.

It never answers for you what only you can answer. Salary, notice period, visa, relocation, and every legal declaration ("I certify that…") are left to you. This is enforced in code, not just requested of the AI.

It never sends an application behind your back. The form is filled in your own browser and stops there. Autopilot can go through many jobs in one go and, only if you switch it on, press submit when nothing is left to check.

It never sees your job-site passwords. You sign in to Naukri or LinkedIn once, the normal way, in your Chrome. ApplySarthi applies through your own session.

Applying and interviewing, from one place

ApplySarthi

Finds the jobs, tailors your CV, fills the forms. Web app; the form filling needs Google Chrome on a computer.

Interview Sarthi

When an interview comes, live help on the call in English, Hindi or Hinglish, grounded in the same CV. Windows app.

Both run on your own free Google Gemini key, so one key does everything and nobody else's usage limits yours. Mark a job as Interviewing in ApplySarthi and it points you to Interview Sarthi for the next step. If you do not have a key yet, the two-minute guide walks you through it.

What you need

  • A CV as PDF or Word.
  • A free Google Gemini API key (how to get one). Your CV reading, tailoring and form filling run on it.
  • Google Chrome on a laptop or desktop for the form filling. The job list and your tailored CVs work on any device.

Questions people ask

Is it really free?

Yes, while in early access. The AI runs on your own Gemini key, which Google offers free within its limits, so ApplySarthi has no per-use cost to pass on. If paid plans come later, they will be one-time passes like Interview Sarthi's, never a subscription that renews by itself.

Will recruiters be able to tell?

There is nothing to tell. Your CV is your real CV, reworded to match the job the way a careful candidate would. Applications go through your own accounts, from your own browser. The point is fewer generic applications, not more of them: the app skips jobs whose title is a different kind of work, even when the skills overlap.

How many jobs can I apply to a day?

As many as you can check. LinkedIn limits how many Easy Apply applications an account may send in a day, so keep that one small; company career pages have no such limit.

Where is my data kept?

Your CV, the tailored versions and your answers are stored in your account on ApplySarthi's server and are yours alone; you can delete the account and everything in it from the Setup page at any time. Your Gemini key is stored encrypted and never shown again. See the privacy policy.

Does it work on a phone?

You can browse your matches and download tailored CVs on a phone. Filling application forms needs the Chrome extension, which runs on a computer.
