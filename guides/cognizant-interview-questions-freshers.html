Cognizant GenC interview questions for freshers: tiers, process and the answers

GenC is one of the most non-CS-friendly doors into IT — and one where knowing which track you're in changes everything about how to prepare. Here is the process, the money, and the questions.

Know your track before you prepare. The same “Cognizant interview” is a basics conversation at GenC level and a live problem-solving session at the higher tiers. Your assessment performance decides the tier — so if your coding round went well, prepare for the harder interview even if you applied for the standard role.

The technical questions Cognizant actually asks

  • “Walk me through your final-year project” — then one drill question per claim. Know what you built, what breaks under load, and what you'd change today.
  • One language, honestly declared. Java: OOP pillars with real examples, exception handling, collections basics. Python: lists vs tuples, dictionaries, a simple file read. C: pointers and strings. The questions follow whatever your resume claims.
  • SQL, reliably: a join written live, WHERE vs HAVING, primary vs foreign key. A smooth query is the single best impression a services-company fresher can make.
  • Definition ladders: what is DBMS → normalisation in plain words; what is an operating system → process vs thread. Two clean sentences beat a memorised lecture.
  • For higher tracks: live coding — arrays, strings, recursion — with your thinking narrated. Practising out loud matters more than more problems.

The HR questions, Cognizant edition

The generic scripts — relocation, shifts, gaps, other offers — are in the ACC HR round guide. Cognizant-specific notes:

  • “Why Cognizant?” One level above “MNC brand”: “Two reasons. GenC's structured onboarding — training pehle, project baad mein, which suits how I learn. And the healthcare and banking client base: I'd rather learn domains that stay valuable for decades.” Both are true things about Cognizant, so it lands as research, not flattery.
  • Joining-timeline questions. Cognizant asks about your availability and constraints directly; answer plainly and don't over-promise a date your college schedule can't keep. A calm honest answer beats an eager wrong one that surfaces later.
  • Locations include Chennai, Bangalore, Hyderabad, Pune, Kolkata, Coimbatore and more. Same iron rule as every mass recruiter: your spoken relocation answer must match your application form.

What actually rejects people at Cognizant

  • Preparing for the wrong tier — GenC-level prep in a higher-track interview.
  • A resume language you can't code in when asked live.
  • Contradicting the application form on relocation, backlogs or dates.
  • Badmouthing your college, an internship, or another company.
  • Panic silence. Ask for ten seconds, structure, answer — the pause is trainable.

Language: bilingual in practice, like every mass recruiter — follow the interviewer into Hindi if they go there, keep the technical terms in English. The Hinglish answer guide shows the balance.
