The session-cap problem

An Indian job switch is rarely 5 calls. A typical loop is screening + technical + managerial + HR, times two or three companies in parallel, 8 to 12 sessions in a busy fortnight, plus reschedules. On a 5-session monthly plan you're rationing your safety net exactly when you need it most, or paying for an upgrade mid-process. This is the single biggest practical difference: Interview Sarthi has no session meter anywhere, pass active, use it every day if the week demands it.

Where Final Round AI is genuinely better

It's a broader prep platform: AI mock interviews with feedback, a resume builder, a question bank, and session recordings with reports. If what you want is practice infrastructure in English and you're happy with a subscription in dollars, it's a legitimate choice, especially the annual plan if you interview year-round. It also runs in the browser, so Mac users are covered (Sarthi is Windows-only).

Where Interview Sarthi is built differently

Sarthi spends everything on the live moment: hears the call, transcribes the interviewer, drafts the answer from your resume in a couple of seconds, in the language the interviewer used, English or Hinglish. It's invisible in screen share on every plan. There's no account with a card on file, no renewal to remember, and no vendor cloud between you and the AI. Your audio streams from your PC to Google under your own free Gemini key.
