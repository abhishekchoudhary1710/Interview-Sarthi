Practise the TCS NQT interview out loud

Most TCS preparation is a list of questions to memorise. That is not what the panel does. It reads your resume in front of you and asks from there, which means the questions you get are the ones only you can be asked.

The short answer. Many TCS drives compress the technical, managerial and HR rounds into a single panel of roughly 25 to 40 minutes. The panel works from your resume: your final-year project, the languages you listed, the subjects on your degree. The commonest reason people fail is not a hard question. It is stumbling over something they wrote themselves.

What the TCS round actually is

The shape varies by drive. On campus it is usually one panel; off campus the rounds are more often separated. Either way the total speaking time is short, so the first two minutes carry more weight than anything later.

What actually trips people up at TCS

You wrote it, so you have to own it

The panel does not need to invent a hard question. It reads a line from your resume and asks you to explain it. If you listed a language you used once in a semester project, that is fair game. If your project summary says you improved something, expect to be asked by how much and how you measured it.

One panel means switching register mid-conversation

You can be explaining a database schema and be asked, in the next breath, whether you are willing to relocate. People who have only practised technical answers go flat when the tone changes, and people who have only practised HR answers sound rehearsed when the technical question lands.

Short rounds punish rambling

In 25 to 40 minutes a panel may only get through six or seven questions. An answer that takes ninety seconds to reach its point costs you one of them.

A 20-minute practice plan for TCS

Twenty minutes is about half a real TCS panel, which is enough to find out whether your resume answers hold up. Upload the same CV you will actually submit.

  1. Upload your real CV, not a tidied one. The whole point is that the questions come from what the panel will be holding.
  2. Pick English, or Hinglish if that is how the round will actually go. Many TCS panels in India switch language mid-conversation and you should practise the version you will face.
  3. Answer out loud, at speaking pace. Reading an answer you have written is not the same skill.
  4. When a question about your project comes back a second time asking for more, do not repeat yourself. That second ask is the panel checking whether there is anything underneath.
  5. Read the report before you practise again. The pause before your answer and your words per minute are measured from your microphone, and both are things a panel notices without naming.

Questions people ask

How long is the TCS NQT interview?

Where the rounds are combined into one panel, usually 25 to 40 minutes in total. Where the HR round is held separately it runs about 10 to 20 minutes on its own.

Is the TCS NQT interview technical or HR?

Both, and often in the same sitting. Many campus drives put technical, managerial and HR questions to you from one panel, which is why practising only one of the three leaves you exposed.

What does the TCS panel ask from the resume?

Your final-year project in detail, the programming languages you listed, and the core subjects of your degree. Anything you put on the page can be asked about, including numbers you claimed.
