What it will not do

An interviewer, not a flatterer.

It never coaches you mid-interview.

During the call she is an interviewer, not a tutor. The teaching is in the report, where it belongs.

It never invents facts about you.

Questions come from your CV. Stronger answers use only what your CV and your own words established.

It never runs over.

The interview ends the second your time is up, and the report is written from what was covered.

It never sends your CV or voice to us.

They go from your browser straight to Google, on your key. Our server sees only a one-way hash of the key, to count your free minutes. Reports stay in your browser.

Questions people ask

Short answers.

What does it cost?

The first 20 minutes are free, with no card and no sign-up, and inviting a friend earns you 20 more. After that a pass gives you unlimited mocks: Rs 99 for 7 days or Rs 249 for 30 days. One payment through UPI, card or netbanking, and it never renews by itself. The interviewer always runs on your own Gemini key, which Google offers free within its limits.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. It is a web page, not an app to install. It needs a browser with a microphone. Earphones help, because from a loud speaker the interviewer can hear her own voice.

Which languages can the interview be in?

English, Hinglish and Hindi are one tap. Type any other language, or choose "match whatever I speak" and she follows you.

How do I get more free minutes?

Invite a friend from inside the app. When they do their first mock through your link, you both get 20 more free minutes, for your first 5 friends. Your score is never part of the invite message unless you choose to add it.

Can I get a second free trial in another browser?

No. The free minutes belong to your Gemini key, not to the browser, so they are the same everywhere you use that key.

She stopped talking for a few seconds. Is it broken?

Google's free tier sometimes goes quiet mid-call. Prep Sarthi notices, reconnects by itself and carries on from the same question. Your transcript and timer are kept.

My PDF did not read properly.

A scanned CV is a picture, so there is no text to read. Paste the text into the box instead. It works just as well.

How is this different from Interview Sarthi?

Prep Sarthi is for the days before: it plays the interviewer so you can practise. Interview Sarthi is for the day itself: a Windows app that helps you during the real call. One Gemini key works for both.
