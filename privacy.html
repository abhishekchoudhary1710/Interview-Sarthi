Privacy Policy

Last updated 29 August 2026 · Interview Sarthi is operated by an individual seller in India; payments are processed by Dodo Payments Inc as merchant of record.

Short version: the software runs on your computer. Your audio, resume and transcripts go from your PC directly to the AI provider under your key. We run no servers and never receive them.

What the software processes, and where

  • Meeting audio: captured on your PC and streamed to Google Gemini under your own API key for transcription and answers. It is not sent to us.
  • Your resume: converted to text on your PC and stored in %APPDATA%\Interview Sarthi. A compressed summary is sent to the AI provider with each answer request. A scanned PDF is sent once to Gemini to be read.
  • Transcripts and summaries: saved only on your PC. Delete the folder above to remove them.
  • API keys: stored on your PC in config.env. Never transmitted to us.

What we receive

  • Purchase data: name, email, country and payment details are collected by Dodo Payments (our merchant of record) under their privacy policy. We see your name, email and order.
  • License checks: on start-up and about every 10 minutes during a session, the software sends your license key and a device name to Dodo Payments to confirm the pass is valid. Nothing about the interview is included.
  • Support email: whatever you choose to send us, including any problem report you attach.

This website

The website, not the software, uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to count visits and see which parts of the page people read, so we know what to fix. These set cookies and record anonymised page interactions on this site only. They never run inside the app, never see your interviews, and are the only tracking anywhere in the product. Block them with any content blocker if you prefer; the site works the same. Google explains what it does with data from sites that use its tools in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

What we do not do

No ads, no selling or sharing of data, no tracking of any kind inside the software. The desktop app sends nothing to us, not a single event.

Your rights

Email us to see or delete what we hold about you (your order details). For payment records, contact Dodo Payments. Data on your own PC is under your control at all times.

The AI provider

Google's handling of API data is described in the Gemini API terms; on the free tier Google may use API content to improve its services. Use a paid key if that matters for your interviews.
