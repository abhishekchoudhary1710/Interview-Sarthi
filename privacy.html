Privacy Policy

Last updated 25 September 2026 · Interview Sarthi is operated by an individual seller in India. Payments from India are processed by Cashfree Payments. Payments from outside India are sold through Dodo Payments Inc as merchant of record, which was also the case for every order placed before 16 September 2026.

Scope: this policy covers Live Sarthi, Prep Sarthi and this website. Live interview content goes from your PC to Google; Prep interview content goes from your browser to Google; in the free demo, the requests that prepare the interview and write the report pass through our license server on the way, without being stored. We operate licensing and payment services as described below. Apply Sarthi stores account data under its separate privacy policy.

What Live Sarthi processes, and where

  • Meeting audio: captured on your PC and streamed to Google Gemini under your own API key for transcription and answers. It is not sent to us.
  • Your resume: converted to text on your PC and stored in %APPDATA%\Interview Sarthi. A compressed summary is sent to the AI provider with each answer request. A scanned PDF is sent once to Gemini to be read.
  • Transcripts and summaries: saved only on your PC. Delete the folder above to remove them.
  • API keys: stored on your PC in config.env. Never transmitted to us.

What we receive

  • Purchase data: when you buy a pass, your email address and phone number are collected at checkout and stored in our own licensing database with the plan and the order status. Your card, UPI and netbanking details go to Cashfree Payments and are never seen by us. Passes bought from outside India, and every pass bought before 16 September 2026, are sold by Dodo Payments Inc under their privacy policy.
  • License checks: on start-up and about every 10 minutes during a session, the software sends your license key and a device name (for example “DESKTOP-3F2 (Windows)”) to confirm the pass is valid. Keys beginning “IS-” go to our own license server at license.interviewsarthi.com and also carry an anonymous device id, so reinstalling on the same PC takes back the slot it already had. Keys issued before 16 September 2026 are checked against Dodo Payments. Nothing about the interview is included.
  • Your email address, if you give it: setup has an optional email box. Fill it in and the address alone is sent to our server, so we can send your license key if you buy a pass and tell you when features land. Leave it empty and nothing is sent. No account is created either way, and you can ask us to delete it at any time.
  • Support email: whatever you choose to send us, including any problem report you attach.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to measure visits and page interactions. They do not run inside the Live Sarthi desktop app. Prep uses Google Analytics for the events described in its section below; Clarity is disabled inside the Prep app and on receipt pages. Block these tools with a content blocker if you prefer; the website still works. Read how Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

Website measurement includes an AI referral event when a recognised campaign source or referring hostname is present. It records the source category and landing-page path, without query strings. Receipt pages with license keys are excluded from Clarity recording, and receipt query values are removed before analytics loads.

Prep Sarthi

Prep Sarthi, the mock interviewer at interviewsarthi.com/prep, runs in your browser. The report is saved only in your browser's storage, where clearing site data removes it.

The free demo. Your first mock interview runs on Interview Sarthi's own Gemini account, so you need nothing of your own. Your voice and the interviewer's replies travel between your browser and Google directly, using a one-time access token our license server obtains from Google for that interview. The request that prepares the interview (your CV and the job description) and the request that writes your report (the interview transcript) pass through our license server on their way to Google; we do not store or log their contents. The demo uses the free tier of Google's Gemini API, under which Google may use what it receives to improve its services. To keep the demo to one per person, we store a random identifier your browser creates for this purpose, a one-way hash of your network address (never the address itself), when the demo started and how many requests it made.

With a pass. Your CV, the job description, your voice and the interviewer's replies travel between your browser and Google's Gemini on your own API key. They do not pass through our servers, and we cannot see or store them. The report is written the same way.

Where free minutes on your own key apply, the page counts them by sending our license server a one-way SHA-256 hash of your Gemini key and the number of seconds used. The key itself never leaves your browser and cannot be recovered from the hash. We store that hash, the seconds used, and the first and last time it was seen. Nothing else about you or your interview is sent to us, and no account is needed.

Invites. Each key hash gets an invite code. If you arrive through a friend's link, we store which code you came through, so the free minutes and free days reach the right people. The friend who invited you sees only counts, how many friends tried it and how many bought a pass, never who they are.

Passes. To buy a pass you sign in with Google. We receive your Google account's email address, name and Google's identifier for the account, and store them with the time your pass ends. Buying from India you also enter a mobile number, because Cashfree requires one; buying from anywhere else you do not, and we store none. Whichever processor takes the payment, we store the order with your email, the plan and that processor’s payment reference, and email you a receipt. A sign-in session is kept in your browser's storage for up to 30 days. Passes never renew by themselves, so no payment details are kept by us or charged again.

Your Gemini key is kept in your browser's storage only if you leave "Remember on this device" ticked. The Prep Sarthi app page counts visits and steps with Google Analytics (for example that an interview started, its language and length, and the report score) and never records the screen: Microsoft Clarity is switched off inside the app. Google processes what you send it under its own terms; on Google's free tier it may use that content to improve its products, which is Google's rule for free keys and applies to anything you do with one.

ApplySarthi

Apply Sarthi, at apply.interviewsarthi.com, has its own account and privacy policy. That policy explains storage of CVs, application documents and encrypted Gemini keys. This page covers Live Sarthi, Prep Sarthi and this website.

What we do not do

No ads or selling of personal data. The Live Sarthi desktop app has no usage analytics; website and Prep measurement are described above. Apart from the license check and the optional email box described above, the desktop app sends us nothing: no analytics, no usage events, and nothing at all about your interview.

Email we send you

Your purchase email with the license key, and replies to your own support messages, are part of the service and are always sent.

We may also email you occasionally about Interview Sarthi and our other products, such as ApplySarthi. Every one of those carries an unsubscribe line: use it, or reply asking us to stop, and we send no more. We never sell or share your address.

Your rights

Email us to see or delete what we hold about you: your order, your email address and, if you gave one, your phone number. For card, UPI and netbanking records contact Cashfree Payments if you paid from India, or Dodo Payments if you paid from outside India or before 16 September 2026. Data on your own PC is under your control at all times.

The AI provider

Google's handling of API data is described in the Gemini API terms; on the free tier Google may use API content to improve its services. Use a paid key if that matters for your interviews.
