Interview Sarthi vs LockedIn AI: credits, price and what a job hunt actually costs

LockedIn AI markets itself as an all-round career copilot: live interview help, a coding copilot, resume tools. The comparison that matters for an Indian job seeker is simpler: what does the interviewing month cost, in which currency, and is your live help metered? Updated September 2026.

The short version

LockedIn AI is a dollar subscription: the unlimited plan is listed at $49.99/month (≈ ₹4,400), or about $29.99/month if you commit quarterly, and the cheaper plans are credit-based, metering your live minutes. Interview Sarthi is ₹399–1,999 one-time, every pass has unlimited sessions, and nothing auto-renews.

The credit-meter problem

Metered minutes and interviews are a bad match. An Indian job switch is usually several companies in parallel: screening, technical, managerial and HR rounds, 8 to 12 calls in a busy fortnight, each 30–60 minutes, plus reschedules. On a credit plan you are watching a meter during the exact hour you should be watching the interviewer; run out mid-loop and you're upgrading under pressure, in dollars. Interview Sarthi has no meter anywhere, while a pass is active, use it for every call, every day.

Where LockedIn AI is genuinely better

Breadth. If you want one subscription that also covers live coding assessments, general meetings and resume tooling, LockedIn bundles all of it, and its browser-based app covers Mac users (Sarthi is Windows-only). Its free career tools, resume checker, cover-letter generator and the rest, are genuinely free to use. If you interview year-round in English and a ₹2,600–4,400 monthly line item doesn't bother you, it's a serious product.

Where Interview Sarthi is built differently

Sarthi spends everything on the live moment: it hears the call, transcribes the interviewer, and drafts the answer from your resume in a couple of seconds, in the language the interviewer used, English or Hinglish. It's invisible in screen share on every pass, not just an upper tier. And the architecture is different, not just the price: the app runs on your PC with your own free Gemini key, so there is no vendor cloud hearing your interviews, no account, and no card on file that can ever be charged again.
