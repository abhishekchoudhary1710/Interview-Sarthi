Apply Sarthi — consistent listing copy
Official page: https://interviewsarthi.com/apply/
App: https://apply.interviewsarthi.com/
Apply Sarthi brings jobs from multiple sources into one browser app, matches them to your CV, helps tailor documents and fills supported application forms through a Chrome extension. Always free, with no paid plan. Public browsing needs no sign-in or Gemini key. Personal features need an account; further AI tailoring and application help use your own Gemini key.
