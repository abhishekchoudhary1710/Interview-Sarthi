Paid passes include unlimited sessions during their validity. Gemini quotas and any paid AI usage are separate. See purchase options and the refund policy. After the free minutes end, answer generation requires a pass; reinstalling does not reset the trial.

Capabilities and limits

The app transcribes call audio, drafts answers using resume context and supports screen reading. The website describes Teams, Zoom and Google Meet workflows. Try your own audio setup in a mock call before relying on it. Generated answers can be wrong; verify personal claims and technical explanations.

The app requires Windows and an internet connection to Google. It does not include an autonomous mock interviewer or resume builder. Screen-capture exclusions have limits and do not establish permission to use assistance.

Privacy and use

Audio and resume context go from your PC to Google under your key; transcripts are stored locally. The website uses analytics separately from the desktop app. See the privacy policy for the data flows and provider terms.

Use AI assistance only where your employer, interviewer or assessment provider permits it. For preparation, practise in a mock call and review suggestions against your own experience. Read the interview rules guide.
