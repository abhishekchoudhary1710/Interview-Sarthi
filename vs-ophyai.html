Interview Sarthi vs OphyAI: credits, subscription, and what ₹99 buys

Two interview copilots built for candidates in India, sold in completely different ways. One meters your interviews in credits and bills every month. The other sells a pass for a fixed number of days and then stops. Prices below were read off OphyAI's own pages on 10 September 2026.

Disclosure: Interview Sarthi is our product, so read this with that in mind. We have stated OphyAI's plans as their site states them, including the places where they are the better choice. Plans change; check both before paying.

The short version

OphyAI is the more finished product and the wider one: a browser tool, 35 languages, mock interviews, company guides and city pages. It costs ₹2,631 a month on Pro and meters your usage in credits.

Interview Sarthi is narrower and cheaper: Windows only, three languages, no mock-interview suite. A pass costs ₹99 for 48 hours or ₹1,999 for 90 days, one-time, with no meter and nothing to cancel. One month of OphyAI Pro costs more than our entire quarter.

The credit meter is the real difference

Pro gives 300 credits a month. OphyAI's own copy prices a standard or technical session at 15 credits and an advanced one at 25. Taken at face value that is roughly twenty standard sessions a month, or twelve advanced ones, which is plenty for most people.

The trouble with a meter is rarely the number. It is that you are aware of it. A two-company loop is six to ten calls, some of them reschedules, and interview week is exactly when you least want to be working out whether a mock round is worth spending credits on. Our passes have no meter: inside the days you bought, run as many sessions as you like.

Subscription against one-time

OphyAI bills monthly, or annually at ten months for twelve, and keeps billing until you cancel. That is the normal model in this category and there is nothing wrong with it, but a job hunt that drags on quietly keeps charging you, and one that ends still needs you to remember to stop it.

A pass is a different shape. You buy 2, 7, 30 or 90 days. When they run out the app tells you and stops. No card on file, no renewal date, nothing to cancel. Subscription resentment is the loudest complaint in reviews of the American tools in this category, and it is the main reason people write to us.

Where OphyAI is genuinely better

It runs anywhere. It is a browser tool, so it works on a Mac, a Linux laptop, or a machine where you cannot install software. We are Windows only, and if you interview from a MacBook we are simply not an option. This is our biggest gap and no amount of pricing makes up for it.

It covers far more languages. 35 against our three, and it can run the interview in one language while answering in another. If your round is in Tamil, Telugu, Spanish or anything outside our three, they cover you and we do not.

It is a bigger product. Mock interviews, company guides, campus content, city pages. We are a solo build with a narrow job: hear the interviewer, draft the answer, stay out of the share. If you want a practice suite as well as live help, they have one and we do not.

Where Interview Sarthi is built differently

Nothing of yours reaches us. You bring your own free Google Gemini key, and your audio and resume travel from your PC to Google under that key. We run no servers and hold no account for you, so there is no copy of your interview anywhere for us to lose. OphyAI, like every browser copilot, processes through its own service.

Hinglish is the case we built for, not an entry on a list. When the interviewer switches to Hindi mid-sentence the answer switches with them, in Latin script that reads fast under pressure. Thirty-five languages is broader. This is deeper on the one that decides an Indian HR round.

The free tier is a real test. 30 minutes with every feature on, no card and no signup, is enough to run a full mock call and decide. One 15-minute session is enough to see the interface.

How to choose

Start with your laptop. If it is a Mac, this comparison is over and OphyAI wins by default. If it is a Windows machine, ask how long your hunt will run. One interview next week is a ₹99 pass here against a ₹2,631 month there. A long search with rounds in several languages is where their subscription starts to earn its price.

Then use both free tiers on a mock call with a friend before paying either of us. Fifteen minutes of the real thing beats any comparison page, this one included.
