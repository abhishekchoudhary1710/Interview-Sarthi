Practise the Infosys interview out loud

The hard part of the Infosys process is not either round on its own. It is that they often happen within an hour of each other, and they want two different people from you.

The short answer. An online test of 65 questions in 95 minutes, then a technical interview of about 30 to 45 minutes, then HR of about 15 to 30 minutes. At the assessment centre the technical and HR rounds are frequently scheduled on the same day, one after the other.

What the Infosys rounds actually are

The written test is a filter with its own timing pressure. The two interviews are where the decision is made, and their proximity is the thing candidates underestimate.

What actually trips people up at Infosys

The switch from technical to HR

You finish forty minutes of being precise and careful, and then someone asks what you enjoy outside work. Candidates carry the technical register straight into the HR room and come across as cold, or they relax too far and lose the precision when a follow-up technical question arrives late.

Why Infosys, answered honestly

This question is asked at almost every Indian service company and is almost always answered with the same three sentences about a global leader. It is not a trick question, but a generic answer wastes one of the few minutes you get to be a person.

Live coding while speaking

The technical round often asks you to write code and explain it at the same time. People who have practised coding silently find that narrating what they are doing costs them the thread.

A 20-minute practice plan for Infosys

Twenty minutes will not cover both rounds properly, so use it on the join between them, which is what nobody practises.

  1. Upload your CV and add the Infosys job description if you have it.
  2. Start on your project and the core subjects, and answer as you would in the technical round.
  3. When the questions turn personal, let your delivery change with them. Warmer, slower, first person.
  4. Have a real answer for why Infosys. One specific thing, not three general ones.
  5. Check the report for your words per minute. People speed up when they are uncomfortable, and the HR round is where that shows.

Questions people ask

Are the Infosys technical and HR interviews on the same day?

Often, yes. At the assessment centre they are frequently scheduled back to back, which is why practising only one of the two leaves you unprepared for the second.

How long is the Infosys technical interview?

Usually 30 to 45 minutes, covering data structures, DBMS, operating systems, OOP and networking alongside your project. Live coding is common.

What does the Infosys HR round ask?

Tell me about yourself, why Infosys, where you see yourself in five years, and whether you are willing to relocate. It runs about 15 to 30 minutes and is judged on behaviour and communication.
