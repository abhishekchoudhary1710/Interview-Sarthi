Practise the Cognizant GenC interview out loud

Cognizant hires freshers into more than one track, and they do not pay the same. The interview is where you are sorted, which makes it one of the few fresher rounds where performing well is worth money rather than just an offer.

The short answer. Cognizant runs an assessment, then a technical interview of roughly 25 to 40 minutes, then HR. The technical round leans hard on your project: not what it was, but how deep you can go into it and what you did when it broke. That depth is what separates GenC from the higher tracks.

What the GenC round actually is

The assessment gets you into the interview. The interview decides the track. Candidates consistently report the same two questions carrying the most weight, and neither is a puzzle.

What actually trips people up at Cognizant

Explain a project you have worked on, in detail

The words in detail are doing the work. A summary gets you a follow-up, and the follow-up is where people run out. Most candidates can describe what their project did. Far fewer can say why they chose that approach over the obvious one, or what they would change now.

Describe a technical challenge you encountered and how you resolved it

This is the question that sorts the tracks, and it is the one people answer worst. A challenge that was really a typo does not count. The answer needs a real problem, what you tried that did not work, and how you knew when it was fixed.

Core subjects come through the project, not cold

You are less likely to be asked to define normalisation than to be asked why your schema looks the way it does. Revising definitions without being able to apply them to your own work leaves you answering a different question from the one asked.

A 20-minute practice plan for GenC

Twenty minutes is about one GenC technical round. Spend it on the two questions that decide the track rather than on breadth.

  1. Upload the CV that carries your final-year project. If the project is thin on the page, the interview will be thin too.
  2. Add the job description if you have the GenC posting. The questions narrow to the role.
  3. When you are asked about the project, go past the summary before you stop. Say the decision you made and why the alternative was worse.
  4. Have one real failure ready. Not a disaster, just something that did not work the first time and what you did about it.
  5. Read the report. It marks every answer out of 10 and shows a stronger version of what you said, which is the quickest way to see where a track-deciding answer fell short.

Questions people ask

How long is the Cognizant GenC technical interview?

Usually 25 to 40 minutes. Candidates report a conversation built around their project rather than a fixed question list.

What decides GenC, GenC Pro or GenC Elevate?

The assessment gets you through; the interview largely decides the track. Depth on your own project and a credible account of a technical problem you solved are what candidates consistently report separating the tiers.

What does Cognizant ask about the project?

What it did, then why you built it that way, then what went wrong and how you fixed it. The follow-up matters more than the summary.
