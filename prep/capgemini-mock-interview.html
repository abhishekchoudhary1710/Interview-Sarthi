Practise the Capgemini interview out loud

Capgemini is the one fresher process where reading your answers instead of saying them will genuinely cost you. There is a round that scores how you speak, and a technical round that walks your project line by line.

The short answer. An online assessment in four parts, including an AI-proctored English communication round and a game-based cognitive test, taking two to three hours. Then a coding round of two problems in about 45 minutes, a technical interview of 30 to 60 minutes, and an HR round of 10 to 20 minutes.

What the Capgemini rounds actually are

This is a longer process than most of its peers, and the parts that are unusual are the parts people prepare least for.

What actually trips people up at Capgemini

Spoken English is a scored round, not a formality

Most fresher processes judge your English implicitly, inside an interview about something else. Capgemini has a round for it. That changes how you should prepare: fluency under time is the skill being measured, and it is not one you can revise for silently the night before.

Line by line means line by line

Candidates report the technical panel going through the project on the page one item at a time. A project you contributed to but cannot fully account for is worse than a smaller one you built alone.

A remote round has its own failure modes

The technical interview is often on Teams or Webex. Talking over the interviewer because of lag, or going silent while you think and leaving the panel looking at a frozen face, are both things that only happen on a call and are both things you can practise away.

A 20-minute practice plan for Capgemini

This is the company where the free 20 minutes is closest to being the real thing, because the skill being tested is speaking.

  1. Upload the CV with the project you will be asked about, and add the job description if you have it.
  2. Choose English. If the communication round is what worries you, this is the round to rehearse.
  3. Answer out loud in full sentences. Do not summarise, and do not stop at the first full stop.
  4. Let the interviewer interrupt you. On a real Teams call it will happen, and recovering from it gracefully is half the round.
  5. Read the report carefully. Filler words, words per minute and the pause before you start are exactly what a communication round is scoring, and they are measured from your microphone rather than guessed.

Questions people ask

Does Capgemini test spoken English?

Yes. The online assessment includes an AI-proctored English communication round as one of its four parts, which makes Capgemini unusual among fresher processes in scoring speech on its own.

How long is the Capgemini technical interview?

Usually 30 to 60 minutes, often held on Teams or Webex, and built around your projects, OOP concepts, SQL joins and pseudocode.

Can I use Python in the Capgemini coding round?

Not always. C, C++ and Java are the usual options and Python has been restricted in some recent drives, so check the rules on the posting you applied to.

Can I practise a Capgemini interview for free?

Yes. Prep Sarthi gives 20 minutes free with no sign-up and measures your pace, pauses and filler words from your microphone, which is what a communication round is looking at.
